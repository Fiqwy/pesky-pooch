Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 April 2026

1. Who We Are

Pesky Pooch (ABN 91 805 492 076) is a professional dog training, boarding, and pet care business operated by Nikki Logan, based in Gilston on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia. We have over 20 years of experience working with dogs and their owners.

2. What Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, phone, or email, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Your message or enquiry details
  • Details about your dog (breed, age, behavioural concerns)
  • Your address (if relevant to home visit services)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry about our services
  • Book and schedule training sessions, boarding, or other services
  • Provide tailored advice for your dog's needs
  • Follow up on completed sessions

4. Third-Party Processing

We use a third-party automation service (Applied Intelligence) to process enquiries. Your information may be processed using AI systems to assist with our response. Your data is handled securely and is not sold to any third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for up to 2 years from the date of your last interaction with us, or until you request its deletion — whichever comes first.

6. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you believe your privacy has been breached

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
